## List every packed piece

**Planning scenario: six pool tables and accessories from Guangzhou.** Start with the supplier’s package list. Record how the tables and accessories are packed, whether components travel separately, and the handling instructions for each unit.

|Ask the supplier for|Why it helps|
|---|---|
|Numbered packages and external length × width × height|Shows the space required after crates or pallets are added|
|Gross weight of each package|Supports the loading and equipment review|
|Stacking limits and required orientation|Keeps the plan aligned with the packaging|
|Handling instructions and packing photographs|Identifies support points and any protection needed|

Use the finished package measurements, including the crate or pallet. Confirm fragile components and safe handling with the supplier before booking.

## Connect the arrival point to the Oman import arrangements

SOHAR lists container and general-cargo facilities. Use the proposed arrival point together with the final receiving city and site when reviewing the route. [SOHAR port facilities](https://soharportandfreezone.om/stevedoring-services/)

Oman Customs uses Bayan for declarations. Prepare invoices, origin documents and the export-country declaration listed in its English service guidance, with permits where applicable; confirm the final document list with the importer. [Oman Customs declaration service](https://www.customs.gov.om/en/business-services/procedural_services/request-to-apply-a-custom-declaration/)

The official requirements search covers permits, restrictions and approvals. Check against the actual goods and classification as part of the import preparation. [Oman Customs requirements search](https://www.customs.gov.om/en/search-import-and-export-requirements/)

Compare collection and packing in China, export handling, freight, destination handling, clearance, applicable duties and taxes, transport to site, unloading and insurance. Identify each included or excluded item, the responsible party, and how waiting or storage would be charged if needed.

## Prepare the receiving site before dispatch

Tell us whether the truck can reach the unloading point, what equipment is available and who will receive the goods. Include door widths, floor level or access restrictions when the goods need to move indoors.

Agree separately on unloading, movement inside the building, unpacking and installation. This gives the receiving team a clear plan for the work after the vehicle arrives.

## Questions before booking

**Can six pool tables fit in one container?**
Use the supplier’s final package list and loading constraints to answer this. Confirm the number of packed units, their external dimensions and whether they can be stacked.

**What should the supplier confirm about a wooden crate?**
Ask about internal support, restraint, moisture protection where needed and safe lifting points. Confirm the packaging material and any applicable import requirements with the parties preparing the shipment.

**What should I check about insurance and damage records?**
Share the cargo value and review coverage, exclusions, deductible and the claims process before dispatch. Keep packing photographs and package-condition records so any reported damage can be assessed.

**Can I start before the packing details are final?**
Yes. Send the product list, supplier location, delivery city and purchasing stage. Mark missing weights and dimensions as awaiting the supplier, then update them when the packaging is ready.
