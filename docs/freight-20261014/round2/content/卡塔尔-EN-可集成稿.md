## Turn several supplier orders into one clear shipment list

For hats, accessories or other commercial goods bought from several suppliers, a shared list keeps each delivery connected to its order. Confirm the receiving instructions before suppliers dispatch, then record what has arrived and what is still due.

|Prepare for each supplier|Use it to confirm|
|---|---|
|Order reference and goods description|Which products belong to each order|
|Carton marks and expected package count|Which deliveries have arrived and whether there are differences|
|Ready date and delivery location in China|Whether to wait for all goods or review separate dispatches|
|Packed dimensions and gross weight|The final cargo data used to review transport options|

If one supplier is ready earlier, compare waiting and storage with the cost of separate shipments. Keep the supplier and product references when packages are combined.

## Prepare the Qatar delivery side early

Confirm the importer’s customs-code arrangements and keep mixed products separately identifiable in the packing information. This helps connect your supplier list to the import documents. [Qatar Customs guidance](https://www.customs.gov.qa/English/Procedures/UnifiedGuide/Pages/Importation.aspx)

Al Nadeeb offers trader and customs-broker guidance. Agree who handles the declaration and which importer details they need. [Al Nadeeb guides](https://customs.gov.qa/Arabic/AlNadeeb/Pages/UserManual.aspx)

Hamad Port handles containers and general cargo. For a sea-freight quotation, identify the arrival point and final receiving address separately, then confirm the onward delivery arrangements. [Mwani Qatar: Hamad Port](https://www.mwani.com.qa/English/Ports/HamadPort/Pages/default.aspx)

## Compare quotes against the same delivery scope

Review these items together: collection from suppliers, receiving, storage, inspection or repacking, export handling, international freight, destination handling, clearance, duties and taxes, final delivery and insurance. The quotation should identify what is included, excluded or still to be confirmed, with the responsible party for each item.

## Questions before you ship

**Can several 1688 suppliers send goods for one shipment?**
Start with each supplier’s location, ready date and packing details. Confirm the receiving address, carton marks and checking or repacking services, then decide which goods will ship together.

**Will a receiving check cover every item inside the cartons?**
Choose the inspection level your order needs. Package counts and label checks cover the receiving step; opening cartons, counting items and testing products should be specified separately.

**Does door delivery include every charge?**
Compare the named delivery point, import handling, taxes and final delivery against the quotation. If you need a DDP arrangement, include it in your brief so it can be reviewed for the goods and importing party.

**My order is small or the measurements are not ready. Can I start?**
Send the information you have, including the purchasing stage. Ask how minimum charges affect the shipment; final packing data can be added when available.
